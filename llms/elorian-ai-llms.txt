## What Elorian does

- Foundation models for visual reasoning and spatial understanding, aimed at architecture,
  automotive, manufacturing, and robotics.
- Stated goal: "visual AGI" — models that think in images rather than in text-converted
  descriptions of images.
- Founded by Andrew Dai (CEO, ex-Google Brain/DeepMind), Yinfei Yang (Chief Multimodal
  Architect, ex-Apple/Google Research), and Seth Neel (ex-Harvard); Dustin Tran (ex-xAI)
  is Chief Reasoning Architect.
- Raised a $55M seed round at a reported $300M valuation, with Nvidia and Menlo Ventures
  named as strategic partners.
